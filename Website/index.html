Our Parish

St. Stephen Parish in Darkuman, Accra has been serving the spiritual needs of the community since its establishment. Our parish is dedicated to fostering a deep relationship with Christ through worship, education, and service.

We welcome all who seek to deepen their faith and join our community in prayer and service.
