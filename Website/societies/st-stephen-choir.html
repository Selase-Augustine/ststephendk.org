About St. Stephen Choir

Learn about our history, mission, and activities

Who We Are

The St. Stephen Choir is the main parish choir that enhances liturgical celebrations through music. We lead the congregation in worship through sacred music, hymns, and songs that reflect our Catholic faith and traditions.

Choir Information

  • Founded: 1982
  • Founded at: Royal International School
  • Current President: Mr. Sampson Appianing
  • Repertoire: Sacred music, hymns, and liturgical songs
  • Practice Schedule: Weekly

Our Mission

The St. Stephen Choir is dedicated to enhancing the liturgical celebrations of our parish through beautiful music that inspires prayer and worship. We strive to lead the congregation in active participation in the Mass through music that reflects our Catholic faith and traditions.

Our History

The choir was formed in 1982 at the Royal International School, where the Catholic community members were then worshipping at the premises.

Our Pioneers

Some of the pioneers who helped establish the St. Stephen Choir include:

  • The late Mr. Ndede
  • The late Mrs. Christiana Nugent
  • The late Ms. Agnes Mensah
  • The late Mr. Anthony Nuvor
  • Mr. Foster Agyemang

Mrs. Cecilia Aikins Mensah presented the Choir with second set of robes in 1988, which helped establish the choir's visual identity during liturgical celebrations.

Over the years, the choir has grown in both membership and repertoire, becoming an integral part of the parish's liturgical life. The choir continues to enhance the worship experience through sacred music that inspires prayer and reflection.

Join St. Stephen Choir

We welcome all parishioners who have a love for music and a desire to serve the parish through song. No prior musical experience is required - just a willingness to learn and commit to regular practice. To join or learn more about our activities, please contact the parish office or speak with any current member.
