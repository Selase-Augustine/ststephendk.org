Brief History of the Parish
The St. Stephen Catholic Church is an offshoot of the Holy Family Parish, Mataheko. The idea to establish a Church in the Darkuman Community originated from a crusade launched in 1977 by the then Parish Priest of the Holy Family Parish, Rev. Fr. Paul Van Riel, an SVD, for the spread of the Catholic faith in the suburbs of the city of Accra. At that time the Holy Family Parish served as a central point where the faithful from the suburbs converged to worship. This served as a driving force for the establishment of community-based churches in areas such as Darkuman, Kaneshie, Dansoman, Odorkor to afford easy access to worship, propagation of the Gospel and spread of the Catholic faith. A few parishioners resident at Darkuman responded positively to the call as it did happen in other communities mentioned above. Mr. Stephen Ocloo, led the crusade to form the St. Stephen's Catholic Church in March, 1978. It is worthy of note that the name "Stephen" was given to the Church in 1982, in honour of two of its prominent personalities in the person of Mr. Stephen Ocloo and Stephen Kwame Kersi. The pioneers included Mr. Stephen Ocloo – Leader, Mr. Raphael Ocloo, Mr. Alex Atitsu, Mr. Anthony Nuvor (deceased), Mr. John Ampah, Mad. Cecilia Ocloo, Mad. Veronica Amuzu, Mad. Juliana Amponsah and Seven Children.

The land on which the parish sits was donated by Mr. Stephen Kersi. The Mission House and Nursery School lands were later purchased.

Over the years, the church has been under the leadership of the following priests; Rev. Fr. Charles Rosaline (1981-1982), Msgr. Anthony Balee (1982-1992), Archbishop Emeritus Thomas Mensah (1992-1995), Msgr. Francis Bernard Ocran (1995-2000), Rev. Fr. Andy Soley (2000-2005), Rev. Fr. Henry Frempong (2005-2009), Msgr. Francis Adoboli (2009-2012), Rev. Fr. Dr. Eric Oduro-Wiafe (2012-2016), Rev. Fr. Bonaventure Quaidoo (2016-2020), Rev Fr. Robert Billy Gbettey (2020-2025).

The lay faithful have been since the establishment of the church been under the chairmanship of the following: Mr Samuel Kwesi Newman (1983-1987), Mr Peter Akwetea (1987-1990), Mr Mathew Kofi Koomson (1990-1995), Mr Albert Adubofour (1995-1997), Mr Kofi Demitia (1997-2001), Mr Edward Taylor (2001-2006), Mr John Cofie Agama (2006-2008), Mr Dennis Aggrey-Ampiah (2008-2010), Mr John Kuma Cato (2010-2015), Mr Alfred Koomson (2015-2016), Mr Thomas Ennin (2017-2019), Mr John Bosco Kru (2019-2022), Mr Maxwell Quarm (2022-2024) and Mr Kofi Atta Yorke (2025 to date).

The parish has produced the following priests; Rev. Fr. Stephen Kofi Sapaku, Rev. Fr. Athanasius Agyarko Afful (Cape Coast Archdiocese), Rev. Fr. Derrick Dowouna-Hammond, Rev. Fr. James Gyekye and Rev. Fr. Clement Quagraine. The Parish also produced Rev. Sr. Magdalene Agyapong.
