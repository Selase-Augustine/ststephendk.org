Parish Overview

The St. Stephen Catholic Church is an offshoot of Holy Family Parish, Mataheko. The community in Darkuman was formed in March 1978 after the missionary outreach that encouraged Catholic worship closer to the suburbs of Accra.

The parish land was donated by Mr. Stephen Kersi, while the Mission House and Nursery School lands were later purchased. Over time the community grew in worship, pastoral organization, education, and infrastructure.
