Uniting Akan-speaking parishioners since 1984

About Akan Nkabom Kuo

Learn about our history, mission, and activities

Who We Are

The Akan Nkabom Kuo is a cultural and spiritual group that celebrates Akan traditions while fostering Catholic faith among Akan-speaking parishioners at St. Stephen Parish, Darkuman.

Society Information

  • Founded: June 24, 1984
  • Founder: Mr. John Aikins-Mensah
  • Initial Membership: 114 members
  • Inaugurated: December 23, 1990
  • First President: Mr. John Aikins-Mensah (1984-2000)
  • Patron Saints: Virgin Mary and St. Joseph
  • Leading Light: Infant Jesus
  • Motto: Unity (as espoused by the Holy Family)
  • Feast Day: Holy Family Day
  • Meeting Schedule: Monthly

Our Mission

The Akan Nkabom Kuo aims at uniting all parishioners of Akan origin in the Church into one formidable Christian Organization and to assist members to participate in the Mass and other liturgical celebrations.

It also aims at promoting true Catholic faith and practice among others by encouraging non-communicants to become communicants and to strictly enforce Christian marriages within the society.

Our History

The Akan Nkabom Kuo was formed on 24th June, 1984 by Mr. John Aikins-Mensah with one hundred and fourteen members. He served as its President from this date until the year 2000. Its Patron Saints are Virgin Mary and St. Joseph her husband with the infant Jesus as the leading light of the society. Its motto is Unity (as espoused by the Holy Family). Its feast day is the Holy Family Day in the year. The society was inaugurated on 23rd December, 1990.

10th Anniversary Celebration - December 29, 1996

The Akan Kuo celebrated its 10th Anniversary on 29th December, 1996 and out-doored its official cloth named "Metwer Botan."

15th Anniversary & First Mass Wedding - September 29, 2001

During its 15th Anniversary activities in 2001, the Akan Kuo organized the first Mass Wedding in the Church on 29th September, 2001 involving thirteen couples (all Akan Kuo members). Rev. Fr. Andy Soley, the then Priest-in-Charge officiated the celebration and was assisted by his predecessor Rev. Msg. Francis Bernard Ocran.

St. Cecilia's Guild

The St. Cecilia's Guild, formed on 24th May, 1985 under the leadership of Mr. Joseph Bonney, is the singing group of the Akan Nkabom Kuo in the St. Stephen Catholic Church. The Guild sings in Akan during Mass and has an active membership of fifty. It is supported financially by the Akan Kuo.

Join Akan Nkabom Kuo

We welcome all parishioners of Akan origin who wish to celebrate their faith through their cultural heritage. To join or learn more about our activities, please contact the parish office or speak with any current member.
