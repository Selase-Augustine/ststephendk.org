Catholic Men's Confraternity

Service to the Church, Help to the Needy

About Catholic Men's Confraternity

The Catholic Men's Confraternity at St. Stephen Parish is a dedicated group of Catholic men committed to serving the Church and helping the needy, as reflected in our motto: "Service to the Church, Help to the Needy."

Since our formation, we have played a pivotal role in the developmental projects of the church and resourced some needy people. We also work to expand our vision by helping establish the Catholic Men's Confraternity in other Catholic Churches.

Our Mission

The Catholic Men's Confraternity is committed to:

  • Providing service to the Church through active participation in parish activities and projects
  • Helping the needy through charitable works and resource mobilization
  • Promoting spiritual growth and development among Catholic men
  • Supporting the expansion of the Catholic Men's Confraternity to other parishes
  • Fostering brotherhood and solidarity among Catholic men

Our History

The Catholic Men's Confraternity was founded in August 1996 through the leadership and initiative of the late Matthew Kofi Koomson who became the first President of the society. Before its inauguration, a working committee was set up to oversee the formation of the society, the drafting and promulgation of the constitution and bye-laws.

After series of discussions and consultations on the constitution and formation modalities, Rev. Fr. Otoo informed the working committee that Most Rev. Dominic Kwodwo Andoh, the then Metropolitan Archbishop of Accra, had given his consent for the formation of the Catholic Men's Confraternity.

Accordingly, the society was inaugurated into the Catholic Church as Catholic Men's Confraternity at St. Stephen Catholic Church, Darkuman on Sunday, August 17, 1997 by Rt. Rev. Monsignor Ocran (Priest-In Charge) with 39 members including 8 executives.

Since its formation, the society has played a pivotal role in the developmental projects of the church and resourced some needy people. These efforts are embedded in the motto of the society "Service to the Church, Help to the Needy".

The society has also inaugurated the Catholic Men's Confraternity in other Catholic Churches such as St. Benedict, Ofankor, Prince of Peace, Kwashieman to help fuel the vision and pool the resources of the men to support the church materially, physically and spiritually.

The current President of the Catholic Men's Confraternity is Alfred Koomson, the son of the Founder.

Join Catholic Men's Confraternity

We welcome all Catholic men who are interested in serving the Church and helping the needy.
