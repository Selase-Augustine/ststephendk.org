About Catholic Students Union (CASU)

The Catholic Students Union (CASU) at St. Stephen Catholic Church is one of the vibrant and dynamic youth groups in the Church, bringing together Catholic students under one umbrella for effective and efficient coordination of activities.

CASU organizes holistic programmes to address the basic needs of students and provides a platform for Catholic students to grow in faith while pursuing their academic goals.

Our Mission

The Catholic Students Union is committed to:

  • Bringing all Catholic students together under one umbrella for effective coordination
  • Organizing holistic programmes to address the basic needs of students
  • Promoting academic excellence while maintaining strong Catholic values
  • Providing spiritual support and guidance to students
  • Creating a community of faith among Catholic students

Our History

The idea to form a Students' Union was conceived by Martin Kumedzro, Raphael Akwetea, and Peter Kersi. Consequently, the Catholic Students Union, which is made up of students, was founded in August 1987.

Mr. Edward Taylor was elected as the first President of CASU. The other founding members include Emmanuel Mensah, Bertha Konadu-Basoa, Anastasia Sakyiamah, Adolf Akwetea, Anthony Akwetea, and Rita Akwetea.

CASU has been one of the vibrant and dynamic youth groups in the Church since its formation, consistently working to support Catholic students in their academic and spiritual journey.
