About Catholic Charismatic Renewal

The Catholic Charismatic Renewal at St. Stephen Parish is a vibrant spiritual movement that emphasizes the experience of the Holy Spirit and the gifts of the Holy Spirit in the life of the Church.

Our group organizes Life in the Spirit Seminars, Crusades, Formations, Revivals, and All-night sessions to help parishioners deepen their relationship with God through the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Mission

The Catholic Charismatic Renewal is committed to:

  • Fostering a personal relationship with Jesus Christ through the power of the Holy Spirit
  • Promoting the use of charismatic gifts for the building up of the Church
  • Providing spiritual formation through Life in the Spirit Seminars and other programs
  • Organizing prayer meetings, revivals, and crusades to renew the faith of parishioners
  • Supporting the parish through various ministries including Healing and Deliverance, Leading, Teaching, Counselling, and Music

Our History

The Charismatic Renewal was formed by Mr. Stephen Ato Burns, who received inspiration from Mr. Cornelius Ahiagbebu of St. Charles Lwanga Parish – Abeka. Together with Mr. Paul Nuvor, Ms. Josephine Quansah, Ms. Theresa Simpson and others, they organized an open-air crusade on the Church grounds, which led to the formation of the group in 1988.

Mr. Burns became the first coordinator of the group under Rev. Msgr. Anthony Balee's guidance and played a major role in nurturing the group. The Renewal has been able to organize Life in the Spirit Seminars, Crusades, Formations, Revivals, and All-night sessions.

The Renewal can also boast of ministries like Healing and Deliverance, Leading, Teaching, Counselling, and Music Ministries, which are performing effectively in the group. Mr. John Bosco Kru is the current Coordinator.

Join Catholic Charismatic Renewal

We welcome all parishioners who are seeking a deeper experience of the Holy Spirit in their lives.

If you would like to join the Catholic Charismatic Renewal, please contact the parish office or speak with any of our members after Mass. You can also attend our weekly prayer meetings to experience the charismatic spirituality firsthand.
