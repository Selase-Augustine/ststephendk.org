About Christian Mothers' Association

The Christian Mothers' Association at St. Stephen Catholic Church is dedicated to developing a deep spiritual life among its members and promoting Christian values in the home and community.

Our association has been instrumental in nurturing the faith of mothers and families, and we are proud to have given birth to the Christian Daughters, a vibrant youth society in the church.

Our Mission

The Christian Mothers' Association is committed to:

  • Developing a deep spiritual life among its members
  • Educating members through seminars, workshops, and conferences
  • Promoting Christian values in the home and family life
  • Carrying out charitable works to help those in need
  • Supporting the spiritual and social development of young women through the Christian Daughters

Our History

The Christian Mothers' Association was formed in the Church in 1983 and inaugurated in 1985 with Mrs. Cecilia Aikins-Mensah as its first President.

Since its formation, the association has been dedicated to developing a deep spiritual life among its members and educating them through seminars, workshops, and conferences. The association has also carried out many charity works over the years.

One of the association's greatest achievements was giving birth to the Christian Daughters, a vibrant youth society in the church that continues the mission of nurturing young women in faith.

The current president of Christian Mothers is Mrs. Ellen Asare, while Mrs. Genevieve Apau serves as the president of the Christian Daughters.

Join Christian Mothers' Association

We welcome all mothers and women who are committed to deepening their spiritual life and promoting Christian values in their families and community.
