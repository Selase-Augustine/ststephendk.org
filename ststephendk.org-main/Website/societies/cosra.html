About COSRA

The Catholic Organization for Social and Religious Advancement (COSRA) at St. Stephen Catholic Church is dedicated to promoting social and religious advancement within our church community and beyond.

Throughout the years, COSRA has played an active role in Church activities as well as in the archdiocese, organizing various programs such as Congresses, visits to Orphanage Homes, and making donations to the needy.

Our Mission

COSRA is committed to:

  • Promoting social and religious advancement within the parish community
  • Organizing programs that benefit both the church and the wider community
  • Supporting the needy through charitable donations and outreach
  • Fostering spiritual growth and development among its members
  • Collaborating with other parish groups to strengthen the church community

Our History

The Catholic Organization for Social and Religious Advancement (COSRA) was formed in 1993 with the help of Mr. Joseph Osei Owusu. Rt. Rev. Thomas Mensah, now the late Emeritus Archbishop of the Kumasi Archdiocese, inaugurated the society on 5th June, 1994.

Throughout these years, the society has played an active role in Church activities as well as in the archdiocese. They have organized series of programs such as Congresses, visits to Orphanage Homes, and made donations to the needy.

The current president is Mr. Daniel Amfoh, who continues to lead the organization in its mission of social and religious advancement.
