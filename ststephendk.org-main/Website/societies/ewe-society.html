Who We Are

The Ewe Society is a cultural and spiritual group that celebrates Ewe traditions while fostering Catholic faith among Ewe-speaking parishioners at St. Stephen Catholic Church, Darkuman.

Society Information

  • Founded: 1985
  • First President: Mr. Kuleke
  • Current President: Mr. Noel Abuobisah
  • Meeting Schedule: Monthly

Our Mission

The main objective of the formation of the society was to unite the Ewes within the community to promote the welfare of members and support the church.

Our History

The Ewe Society was formed in the year 1985. The first President was Mr. Kuleke. The main objective of the formation of the society was to unite the Ewes within the community to promote the welfare of members and support the church.

St. Gabriel Singing Group

The Ewe Society formed the St. Gabriel Singing Group which has featured prominently during the celebration of mass and other landmark events such as Musical Festival, Archbishop's Pastoral visits.

The St. Gabriel Singing Group provides beautiful music, enriching the liturgical celebrations and preserving the cultural heritage of the Ewe community within the parish.

Join Ewe Society

We welcome all parishioners of Ewe origin who wish to celebrate their faith through their cultural heritage. To join or learn more about our activities, please contact the parish office or speak with any current member.
