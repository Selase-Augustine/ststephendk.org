About Knights of St. John International & Ladies Auxiliary

The Knights of St. John International & Ladies Auxiliary at St. Stephen Catholic Church is a fraternal organization dedicated to serving God, the Church, and our community with honor and dedication.

Our organization brings together both brothers and sisters in faith, working together to support the Church's mission and serve those in need in our community.

Our Mission

The Knights of St. John International & Ladies Auxiliary is committed to:

  • Serving God through active participation in Church activities
  • Supporting the Church's mission and pastoral work
  • Providing charitable assistance to those in need
  • Promoting fellowship and brotherhood/sisterhood among members
  • Upholding the values of honor, integrity, and service

Our History

The Knights of St. John International and Ladies Auxiliary was formed and inaugurated on May 16, 2015, with eighteen brothers and twenty-three sisters as founding members.

Mr. Geoffrey Kofi Doagbodzie served as the first President of the organization and continues to lead the group in its mission of service to God, Church, and community.

Since its formation, the organization has been actively involved in various Church activities and community service projects, living up to its commitment to serve with honor and dedication.

Join Knights of St. John International & Ladies Auxiliary

We welcome all Catholic men and women who are committed to serving God, the Church, and the community.

If you would like to join the Knights of St. John International & Ladies Auxiliary, please contact the parish office or speak with any of our members after Mass.
