League of Tarcisian's of the Sacred Heart

Carrying the knowledge and love of Jesus to other souls

About League of Tarcisian's

The League of Tarcisian's of the Sacred Heart at St. Stephen Parish is named in memory of the boy martyr St. Tarcisian, who carried the Blessed Sacrament to early Christians in prison and was stoned to death by prison soldiers.

Like Tarcisian, boys and girls of the League desire to carry the knowledge and love of Jesus to other souls, especially their families.

Our Mission

The League of Tarcisian's is dedicated to:

  • Promoting devotion to the Sacred Heart of Jesus among young people
  • Carrying the knowledge and love of Jesus to other souls, especially their families
  • Fostering a spirit of sacrifice and service among its members
  • Encouraging regular participation in the sacraments

Our History

The League of Tarcisians was formed in 1983 by Mr. Stephen Kwame Kersi. The name "Tarcisian" was taken in memory of the boy martyr St. Tarcisian who carried the Blessed Sacrament to the early Christians in prison and was stoned to death by the prison soldiers.

Since its formation, the League has been instrumental in nurturing the faith of young people in the parish and encouraging them to be active participants in the life of the Church.

Join League of Tarcisian's

We welcome young people who are interested in deepening their faith and sharing the love of Jesus with others.

If you or your child would like to join the League of Tarcisian's, please contact the parish office or speak with any of our members after Mass.
