About The Marshallan Association

The Marshallan Association is a Catholic Friendly Society with the motto: "Unity, Charity, Fraternity and Service." We pledge unqualified loyalty to the Church and are committed to defending it under all circumstances.

Our association brings together Catholic men and women in a spirit of brotherhood and sisterhood, working together to support the Church and serve our community.

Our Mission

The Marshallan Association is dedicated to:

  • Promoting unity among Catholic faithful
  • Practicing charity through service to the needy and community
  • Fostering fraternity and fellowship among members
  • Providing service to the Church and parish community
  • Defending and supporting the Catholic Church under all circumstances

Our History

The Marshallan Association was formed and inaugurated on October 16, 1999, with sixteen founding members. Mr. Adolf Akwetea served as the first President of the association.

Since its formation, the association has remained true to its motto of "Unity, Charity, Fraternity and Service," working tirelessly to support the Church and serve the community.

The current President is Mr. John Kuma Cato, who continues to lead the association in its mission of service to the Church and community.

Join The Marshallan Association

We welcome all Catholic faithful who are committed to unity, charity, fraternity, and service to the Church.

If you would like to join The Marshallan Association, please contact the parish office or speak with any of our members after Mass.
