About St. Anthony's Guild

Learn about our history, mission, and activities

Who We Are

The St. Stephen's branch of the St. Anthony's Guild is a dedicated group of parishioners who follow the spiritual path of St. Anthony of Padua, focusing on service to the church and community.

Society Information

  • Founded: February 14, 1980
  • Founder: Messrs Anthony Yaw Yeboah and John Abeiku Arthur
  • Initial Membership: 25 members
  • Inaugurated: 3rd Sunday of March, 1985
  • First President: Bro. John Abeiku-Arthur
  • Current President: Bro. Edward T. Taylor
  • Meeting Schedule: Monthly

Our Mission

The St. Anthony's Guild is dedicated to promoting devotion to St. Anthony of Padua, supporting the parish through various service activities, and fostering spiritual growth among its members.

Our History

The St. Stephen's branch of the St. Anthony's Guild was formed by Messrs Anthony Yaw Yeboah and John Abeiku Arthur on 14th February, 1980 with an initial membership of twenty-five, and was inaugurated on the 3rd Sunday of March, 1985 by Msg. Anthony Balee, who himself was an Anthonian.

The first President was Bro. John Abeiku-Arthur. Two of its members, Bro. Abeiku-Arthur and Bro. Edward T. Taylor have risen to become Vice President and President respectively of the Church Pastoral Council at various times. The current President is Bro. Edward T. Taylor.

Over the years, the Guild has contributed significantly to the growth and development of St. Stephen Parish through various spiritual and community service activities.

Join St. Anthony's Guild

We welcome new members who are interested in serving the parish community and growing in their devotion to St. Anthony of Padua. To join or learn more about our activities, please contact the parish office or speak with any current member.
