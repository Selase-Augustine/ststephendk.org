About St. Theresa of the Child Jesus Society

The St. Theresa of the Child Jesus Society at St. Stephen Catholic Church is a dedicated group of parishioners who follow the spiritual path of St. Theresa of Lisieux, also known as "The Little Flower."

Our members are committed to living out St. Theresa's "Little Way" - performing small acts of kindness and love with great devotion, recognizing that holiness is found in everyday life and simple gestures.

Our Mission

The St. Theresa Society is dedicated to promoting devotion to St. Theresa of the Child Jesus, supporting the parish through prayer and service, and fostering spiritual growth among its members.

We strive to embody St. Theresa's teachings by performing ordinary tasks with extraordinary love, offering up small sacrifices, and maintaining childlike trust in God's providence.

Our History

The St. Theresa of the Child Jesus Society was founded by nine members in 2002 with the late Mrs. Mary Yankah as its first President. The society currently has a membership of over eighty members.

Since its formation, the society has achieved remarkable milestones including promoting vocation, sponsoring and maintenance of seminarians and aspirants, offering assistance to priests, and sponsoring parish projects.

The current president is Mrs. Charlotte Darko, who continues to lead the society in its mission of following the "Little Way" of St. Theresa through simple acts of love and devotion.

Join St. Theresa Society

We welcome new members who are interested in deepening their faith through devotion to St. Theresa and service to the parish community.

Meetings are held weekly, where we pray together, study the writings of St. Theresa, and plan our service activities.
